See the Explorer platform in action

Explorer is our enterprise genomic analysis platform. It brings cohort discovery, genomic analysis, and research workflows together so teams move from question to answer without waiting for large bioinformatics queues. Our team runs on-demand walkthroughs at the booth for researchers and partners.

Research Access Program

We are broadening access to Helix GenoSphere data for academic researchers worldwide. Selected labs will be able to work with population-scale clinicogenomic records through the Explorer platform.

  • Access to 550,000+ clinicogenomic records with longitudinal clinical linkage
  • Analysis inside Explorer, in a governed research environment
